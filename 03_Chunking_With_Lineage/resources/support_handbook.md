## Accounts
Source: policy_accounts.md

An account is the email plus the customer record. One email, one account.

A customer can change their email once per calendar year. Invoices already issued keep the old email as a snapshot.

Account deletion: we remove the login and the marketing flag. Invoices stay. Support tickets stay for 24 months, then they are dropped. See policy_data_retention.md.

## Cancellations
Source: policy_cancellations.md

A physical order can be cancelled until it ships. After it ships, use the return policy.

A digital order can be cancelled only before the download starts. After the first byte, it is a completed digital sale.

To cancel, write to support with the invoice number the same day if you can.

## Damaged media
Source: policy_damaged_media.md

If a CD or vinyl arrives cracked, warped, or will not play, we replace it at no charge. Photograph the damage. Keep the original copy until the replacement arrives, then recycle it.

If the same title arrives damaged twice, offer a refund instead of a third copy.

## Data retention
Source: policy_data_retention.md

Invoices: kept for 7 years (tax).

Support tickets: 24 months, then deleted.

Download logs: 12 months.

Marketing flags: until the customer opts out or the account is deleted.

We do not keep call recordings. This shop's support is email only.

## Digital downloads
Source: policy_digital_downloads.md

A digital album is available for download for 90 days after purchase. After that the link expires. The customer can request one new link; it lasts 7 days.

Downloads are tied to the customer email on the invoice. We do not send a second copy to a different email.

There is no return and no refund on a completed download unless the file is corrupt. Then we re-issue the link.

## Escalations
Source: policy_escalations.md

Escalate when:

- a refund over 100 dollars is requested
- the same customer has three open tickets
- a representative wants to override a written policy

Escalations go to the support manager. The assigned representative stays on the ticket.

This shop does not publish the manager's personal phone number. Do not invent one.

## Exchanges
Source: policy_exchanges.md

An exchange is a return plus a new order. The customer pays any price difference. If the new item is cheaper, we refund the difference.

Exchanges use the same 30-day unopened window as returns.

We do not exchange a digital album for a vinyl pressing of the same title without a new paid order.

## Gift cards
Source: policy_gift_cards.md

Gift cards are sold in 15, 25, and 50 dollar amounts. They do not expire.

A gift card cannot be exchanged for cash except where the law requires it.

Lost gift-card codes are not replaced. The purchaser should treat the code like cash.

## International orders
Source: policy_international.md

We ship physical goods to the country on the invoice. We do not redirect a parcel in transit.

VAT or sales tax is calculated at checkout for the billing country. We do not add a second tax after the invoice is issued.

Some titles are licensed for one region only. Those cannot be shipped outside that region. The product page says so before purchase.

## Library and playlists
Source: policy_library.md

Purchased digital tracks appear in the customer's library within 15 minutes of a successful payment.

Playlists are stored on the account. Deleting the account deletes playlists. Invoices remain.

We do not move a library from one account to another. That is a new purchase.

## Payment
Source: policy_payment.md

We accept cards and shop gift cards. We do not accept cash by post or cryptocurrency.

A failed payment cancels the order. The customer must place it again.

Split tender (card plus gift card) is allowed. The gift card is applied first.

## Preorders
Source: policy_preorders.md

A preorder can be cancelled until the street date. After the street date it is a normal order.

We do not charge the card until the item ships.

If a preorder is delayed more than 30 days past the advertised street date, the customer may cancel for a full refund.

## Privacy
Source: policy_privacy.md

We store name, billing address, email, and invoice history. We do not store full card numbers after the payment clears.

We do not sell customer lists.

A customer may ask for a copy of their stored data, or ask us to delete the account. Deletion keeps invoices for tax. See policy_accounts.md and policy_data_retention.md.

## Refunds
Source: policy_refunds.md

Refunds go back to the original payment method. They take 5 to 10 business days after support approves the request.

Partial refunds are allowed when only some tracks on a multi-disc set are defective.

We do not refund because a customer changed their mind about a digital purchase.

Duplicate charges: if two invoices exist for the same album on the same day, refund the later invoice in full. Attach both invoice numbers.

## Returns
Source: policy_returns.md

Physical items (CDs, vinyl, boxed sets) may be returned within 30 days of the invoice date if unopened.

Opened physical media can be returned within 14 days only if the disc is defective. A replacement is preferred over a refund.

Digital downloads and streamed albums cannot be returned. See policy_digital_downloads.md.

Start a return by writing to support. Include the invoice number. Support will reply within the SLA in policy_support_hours.md.

## Shipping
Source: policy_shipping.md

Domestic (same country as the billing address): 3 to 7 business days.

International: 10 to 21 business days. Customs fees are the customer's.

We ship physical orders once. We do not split a single invoice across two parcels.

Free shipping on physical orders over 50 dollars before tax.

## Student discount
Source: policy_student_discount.md

A verified student email (ending in .edu or the local equivalent) gets 10 percent off physical items. Digital albums are not discounted.

The discount is applied at checkout. We do not add it after the invoice exists.

The discount cannot be stacked with gift-card promotions.

## Support hours and SLA
Source: policy_support_hours.md

Support is staffed Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in the shop's local time. Closed public holidays.

First reply: within 1 business day.

Refund or replacement decision: within 3 business days of the first reply.

Urgent: damaged media that will not play, or a failed digital download. Those jump the queue.

## Support representatives
Source: policy_support_reps.md

Each customer is assigned one support representative. The assignment lives on the customer record. It does not change when a ticket is opened.

Known representatives in this shop:

- Steve Johnson
- Jane Peacock
- Margaret Park
- Nancy Edwards

A ticket is always answered by the assigned representative when they are in. If they are out, any on-duty representative may reply, then hand it back.

## Warranty on physical media
Source: policy_warranty.md

Manufacturing defects are covered for 90 days from the invoice date. This is separate from the return window.

Wear from ordinary play (scratches, a warped sleeve from sunlight) is not a defect.

We do not warranty third-party players, needles, or headphones sold as add-ons after 30 days.
