# Atlas help: passwords, exports and invoices

Answers to the questions Atlas customers ask most often. For Boreal, see the password reset comparison sheet.

## How do I reset my password?

Atlas passwords can be reset by email. Temporary accounts require a support agent.

## How long does a reset link work?

Atlas reset links expire after 30 minutes. Request a new link if the old link has expired.

## Can I export my reports?

Atlas exports reports as CSV files. Only workspace owners can export all reports.

## Where are my invoices?

Atlas invoices are sent monthly. Workspace owners can download invoices from Billing.
